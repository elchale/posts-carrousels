# Pumpkin Art Co (pumpkinartco)

Source of truth lives OUTSIDE this repo, in `CLIPPING/pumpkin_art/ig/` (posts are generated by
`ig/posts/build_oct.py`, photos + PHOTOS.json licences in `ig/brand/`). Rendered with the
Artist Salon renderer pointed at that folder:
`IG_ROOT=../pumpkin_art/ig <image_gen python> pod_store/tools/render_ig.py oct` and validated with
`IG_ROOT=../pumpkin_art/ig node pod_store/tools/validate_ig.cjs oct`. This folder only mirrors
`posts/<mes>.json` and `out/` (gitignored, served from the bucket); then the usual publish pipeline.

English, US audience. Brand book: `pumpkin_art/BRAND.md`. Cadence Oct 2026: 6 posts on the 2nd,
2 a day from the 3rd to the 9th, 1 a day to the 31st. Oct 2 and 3 are generic Halloween fact posts;
from Oct 4 every post is about a product live in the store.
