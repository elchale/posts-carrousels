== IG ==
Certificate, diploma and record are not interchangeable, and readers notice. Use certificate for a course, diploma for a long programme with real assessment, and record for attendance and hours. Calling a two hour workshop a diploma spends credibility you will want for the programme that deserves the word. Diplomy (diplomy.org) supports each. #certificates #academy #training
