== IG ==
How a student was assessed is the difference between two certificates that otherwise look identical. Written exam, practical test, portfolio review, supervised assessment: name it in one line under the course. Careful readers look for exactly this, and most certificates leave it out. Diplomy (diplomy.org) carries it through to the page. #certificates #assessment #training #academy
