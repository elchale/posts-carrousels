== IG ==
Courses about software, regulation or safety go out of date, and the certificate should be honest about when it was taught. Putting the year on it keeps older certificates valid, they certify what was taught at the time, while giving graduates a clear reason to come back for the current edition. Diplomy (diplomy.org) keeps editions separate. #certificates #training #academy
