== IG ==
Requests for a letter of completion usually want three facts: that the person studied with you, what they studied, and when they finished. A certificate page states all three, is dated, and can be checked by whoever receives it. Send that first and reserve letters for the cases that genuinely need one. Diplomy (diplomy.org) keeps them reachable. #certificates #alumni #academy #admin
