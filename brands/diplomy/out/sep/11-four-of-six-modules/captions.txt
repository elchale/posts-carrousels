== IG ==
Module certificates cover the students who step away mid programme, for reasons that have nothing to do with your course: a job change, a move, a new baby. The modules they finished are real work, and a certificate can say exactly that while claiming nothing about the rest. It also leaves the door open to come back. Diplomy (diplomy.org) certifies modules like courses. Send it to the academy owner you know. #certificates #training #academy

Four of six modules is four modules of real work. Certify those, state them plainly, claim nothing about the rest. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues module certificates.
