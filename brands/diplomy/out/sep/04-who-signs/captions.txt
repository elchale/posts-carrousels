== IG ==
A signature on a course certificate means something only when the reader can tell whose it is. Print the name and the role under it. Two signatures work when the instructor and the school director both stand behind the course, more than two turns into decoration. Diplomy (diplomy.org) stores them with your issuer profile. #certificates #academy #training
