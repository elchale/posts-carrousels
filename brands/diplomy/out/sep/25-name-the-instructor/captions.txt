In trades, therapies and specialised training, students choose the teacher before the school, and readers of the certificate care about the same thing. Naming the instructor and their qualification in one line makes the certificate more specific and gives your best teachers a reason to stay visible. Diplomy (diplomy.org) prints it per course. #certificates #training #academy #instructors
