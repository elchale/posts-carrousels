== IG ==
Attended and completed are different claims, and the people who read your certificates notice which one you make. Attendance certifies presence for the stated hours. Completion certifies a result: an exam passed, a project delivered, a standard met. Say which one it is, every time. Save this for your next batch. Diplomy (diplomy.org) prints either. Send it to whoever answers the verification emails. #certificates #training #academy
