== IG ==
Attended and completed are not the same claim, and mixing them costs you credibility with the people who read your certificates. Attendance certifies that the student was present for the stated hours. Completion certifies a result: an exam passed, a project delivered, a standard met. Say which one it is. Diplomy (diplomy.org) prints either. #certificates #training #academy
