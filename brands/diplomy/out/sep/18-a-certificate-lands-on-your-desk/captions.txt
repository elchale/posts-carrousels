Enrollment season brings certificates to your desk: applicants for advanced levels, transfer students, people placing out of module one. Scan the code or type the printed address, and compare three lines against the paper: name, course and level, issue date. A phone at the front desk covers it. Save this for intake week. Diplomy (diplomy.org) builds pages for exactly that reading. Send it to whoever issues your certificates. #admissions #certificates #academy #enrollment
