== IG ==
Certificates get read by two audiences: the family at home and, sometimes, an employer or an admissions office abroad. The first wants the local language, the second usually wants English. Issuing both from the same record avoids two competing documents for the same course. Diplomy (diplomy.org) handles both versions. #certificates #academy #training #credentials
