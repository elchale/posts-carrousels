== IG ==
Signatures on a course certificate mean something when the reader can tell whose they are. Print the name and the role under each one. Two work when the instructor and the school director both stand behind the course, and a third is decoration. Save this for your next batch. Diplomy (diplomy.org) stores them with your issuer profile. #certificates #academy #training
