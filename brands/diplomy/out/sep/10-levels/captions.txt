== IG ==
A certificate that says English Course tells a reader almost nothing. One that says English Course, level B1, tells them exactly where the student stands, using a scale their field already understands. It also lets graduates stack certificates as they progress instead of replacing one with another. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues one per level. #certificates #languages #training #academy
