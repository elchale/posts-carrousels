== IG ==
Students leave programmes for reasons that have nothing to do with the course: a job change, a move, a new baby. The modules they finished are still real work, and a module certificate says exactly that without claiming the full programme. It also leaves the door open for them to come back. Diplomy (diplomy.org) certifies modules like courses. #certificates #training #academy

== FB ==
Four of six modules is four modules of real work. Certify those, list them plainly, claim nothing about the rest. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues module certificates.
