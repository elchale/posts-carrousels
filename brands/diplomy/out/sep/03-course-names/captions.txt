== IG ==
Your course name travels on every certificate your graduates share, and it is what a recruiter types into a search box. Ten graduates carrying three spellings of the same course look like three different schools. Pick one name, keep the capitalisation, leave version numbers out of the title. Diplomy (diplomy.org) reuses it across intakes. #academy #certificates #training #branding
