Hours are the one field on a course certificate that a stranger can weigh without knowing your school. Forty hours of training reads the same to an employer anywhere in the world. Count class time, guided practice and assessment, and print the total. Save this for your next course setup. Diplomy (diplomy.org) carries it onto the certificate and its page. #certificates #training #cpd #academy
