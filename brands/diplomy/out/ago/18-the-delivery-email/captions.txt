== IG ==
The email that delivers a course certificate is four lines long: the course name, what your student completed, the date, and the certificate link with one line about adding it to their profile. Anything else in there competes with the thing you actually want them to do. Diplomy (diplomy.org) sends it automatically on issue. #certificates #training #academy #edtech
