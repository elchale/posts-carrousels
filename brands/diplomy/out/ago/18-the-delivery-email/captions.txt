The email that delivers a certificate is four lines long: the course name, what they completed, the date, and the link with one line about adding it to their profile. Anything else you put in there competes with the thing you actually want them to do. Diplomy (diplomy.org) sends it automatically on issue. #certificates #training #academy #edtech
