== IG ==
Most graduates would add your course to their profile if someone told them where it goes. The section is licenses and certifications, the issuing organisation is your school, and the credential URL is the certificate link you sent. Put those three lines in the delivery email and the certificate becomes a public mention of the school that ran the course. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues the URL that field asks for. #linkedin #certificates #credentials #academy
