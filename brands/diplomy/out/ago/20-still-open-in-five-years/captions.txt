Certificate longevity is a design decision you make on issue day. A course certificate matters most years later, when a graduate changes jobs or applies for something, and three things keep it usable that long: a stable web address, an open format, and a copy the graduate holds. Save this for your next issue. Diplomy (diplomy.org) ships all three by default. Send it to the academy owner you know. #certificates #openbadges #credentials #alumni
