== IG ==
A course certificate matters most years after it was issued, when someone changes jobs or applies for something. Three things keep it usable that long: a stable web address, an open format, and a copy the graduate holds themselves. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues with all three. #certificates #openbadges #credentials #alumni
