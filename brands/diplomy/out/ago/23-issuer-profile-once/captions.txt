== IG ==
Issuer setup is a twenty minute job you do once, ideally before the fall intake starts: school name, logo and colours, a contact address, and who signs. Every course and every intake after that inherits it, nothing gets rebuilt when a new programme starts. Save this for setup weekend. Diplomy (diplomy.org) applies it automatically to everything you issue. Send it to the person who signs them. #certificates #academy #branding #training
