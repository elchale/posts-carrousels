The issuer profile is a twenty minute job you do once: school name, logo and colours, a contact address, and who signs. Every course and every intake after that inherits it, so nothing has to be rebuilt when a new programme starts. Diplomy (diplomy.org) applies it automatically to everything you issue. #certificates #academy #branding #training
