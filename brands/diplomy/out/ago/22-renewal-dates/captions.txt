== IG ==
Renewal dates belong on the certificate itself. Safety, first aid, food handling and annual refreshers all run on a cycle, and when the certificate page shows the issue date and the renewal date, the holder and their employer both know where they stand without asking anyone. Save this for your next compliance course. Diplomy (diplomy.org) carries the date onto both. Send it to your program coordinator. #compliance #training #certificates #safety
