== IG ==
The criteria line is what separates a certificate that means something from a certificate that looks nice. Name the work, not the topic: hours completed, project delivered, exam passed, sessions attended. One line, printed on the certificate, and anyone who opens it later knows what it took. Diplomy (diplomy.org) carries it onto the verification page too. #certificates #training #academy #credentials #openbadges
