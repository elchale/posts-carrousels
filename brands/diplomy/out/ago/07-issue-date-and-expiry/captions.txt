== IG ==
Two kinds of course, two answers on expiry. A finished course stays finished, so print the issue date and leave it open ended. Training that runs on a cycle, safety, first aid, food handling, compliance, carries a valid until date that the certificate itself should show. Diplomy (diplomy.org) puts it on the certificate and the page. #certificates #compliance #training #credentials

== FB ==
A finished course does not expire. Safety, first aid and compliance training do. Decide which one you are issuing, and let the certificate show the date either way. Diplomy (diplomy.org) handles both.
