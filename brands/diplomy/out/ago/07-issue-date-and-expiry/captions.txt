== IG ==
Two kinds of course, two answers on expiry. A finished course stays finished, so print the issue date on the certificate and leave it open ended. Training that runs on a cycle, safety, first aid, food handling, compliance, carries a valid until date the certificate itself should show. Diplomy (diplomy.org) puts it on both. #certificates #compliance #training #credentials

== FB ==
A finished course does not expire. Safety, first aid and compliance training do. Decide which kind you are certifying, and let the certificate show the date either way. Diplomy (diplomy.org) handles both.
