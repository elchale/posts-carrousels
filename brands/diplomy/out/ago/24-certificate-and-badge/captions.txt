== IG ==
Certificate or badge is not a choice, they do different jobs. The certificate is for reading: a page or PDF your graduate opens, prints, attaches. The "badge" is a small image with the certificate data baked inside, made for a profile or an email signature. Diplomy (diplomy.org) produces both from one issue. Which do your graduates actually share more, the certificate or the badge? Send it to your director before the next intake. #openbadges #certificates #credentials #edtech
