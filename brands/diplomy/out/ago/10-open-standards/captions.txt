== IG ==
Open Badges is the public standard for credentials, which means a certificate issued in it can be read and confirmed by platforms that have never heard of you. For a student that is portability: it opens outside your tools and keeps working if you ever change them. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues in that standard by default. #openbadges #credentials #edtech #training
