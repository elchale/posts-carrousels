== IG ==
Open Badges is the public standard for certificates and credentials, which means one issued in it can be read and confirmed by platforms that have never heard of your school. For a student that is portability: their certificate opens outside your tools and keeps working if you ever change them. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues in that standard by default. #openbadges #credentials #edtech #training
