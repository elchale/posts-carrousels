== IG ==
A certificate and a badge are not competing formats, they are different jobs. The certificate is for reading, opening, printing and attaching to an application. The badge is small, carries the certificate data inside it, and fits where an image fits: a profile, a signature, a portfolio. Diplomy (diplomy.org) produces both from one issue. #openbadges #certificates #credentials #edtech
