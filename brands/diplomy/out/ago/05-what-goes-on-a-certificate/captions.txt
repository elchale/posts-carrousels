== IG ==
A certificate carries four fields everyone remembers, the recipient's full name, what they completed, who issued it and the date. The fifth is the one that makes it useful years later, a link where all four can be opened and confirmed. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues certificates with that link built in. #certificates #openbadges #training #edtech #credentials
