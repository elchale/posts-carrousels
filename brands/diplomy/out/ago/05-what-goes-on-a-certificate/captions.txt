== IG ==
Every course certificate an academy sends carries four fields: who earned it, what they completed, which school issued it, and the date. The fifth is the one that makes it useful years later, a link where all four can be opened and confirmed by whoever your graduate shows it to. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues certificates with that link built in. #certificates #openbadges #training #edtech #credentials
