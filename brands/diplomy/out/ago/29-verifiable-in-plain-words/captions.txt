== IG ==
Verifiable sounds technical and means something simple: whoever is holding the course certificate can confirm on their own that your school issued it, without phoning anyone. The same web address also serves the record to software, which is how a credential travels between platforms. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues that way by default. #credentials #openbadges #certificates #edtech
