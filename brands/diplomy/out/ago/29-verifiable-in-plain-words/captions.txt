== IG ==
Verifiable sounds technical and means something simple: whoever holds a course certificate can confirm on their own that your school issued it, on its "verify page", without phoning anyone. The same address also serves the signed record to software, which is how a credential travels between platforms. Save this for the next time someone asks what it means. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues that way by default. Send it to the person who signs them. #credentials #openbadges #certificates #edtech
