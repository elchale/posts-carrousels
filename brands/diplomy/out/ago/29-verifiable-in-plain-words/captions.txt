== IG ==
Verifiable sounds technical and means something simple: whoever is holding the certificate can confirm on their own that your school issued it, without phoning anyone. The same address also serves the record to software, which is how a credential travels between platforms. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues that way by default. #credentials #openbadges #certificates #edtech
