== IG ==
Bids and framework applications ask training providers the same thing: how many people you trained, in what, when, and what evidence exists that they completed. Providers who issue certificates from one place export that in minutes. The rest spend the two days of notice rebuilding it. Diplomy (diplomy.org) keeps it current. #training #bids #certificates #compliance
