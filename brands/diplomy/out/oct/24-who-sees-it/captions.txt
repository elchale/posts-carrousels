== IG ==
Students ask a fair question about certificate links: who can see this. The honest answer has three lines. Anyone they share the link with, nobody browsing by name, and they choose where it goes. Graduates who feel in control of their record are the ones who share it. Save this for the next time someone asks. Diplomy (diplomy.org) works exactly this way. #privacy #certificates #academy #credentials
