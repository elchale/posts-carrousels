== IG ==
Auditors ask the same four questions of a training record: who was trained, what the course covered, who delivered it, and when. What they judge is consistency, one clean format across every course, rather than the size of the folder. Issuing everything the same way is most of the work. Diplomy (diplomy.org) does that by default. #compliance #audit #training #certificates
