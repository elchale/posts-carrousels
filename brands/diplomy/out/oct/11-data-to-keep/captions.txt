== IG ==
A certificate needs a full name, a course, dates and an email to send it to. Identity numbers, home addresses and phone numbers belong in your enrollment file and nowhere near a page anyone can open. Keeping the certificate minimal is the simplest privacy decision you will make. Diplomy (diplomy.org) publishes only the essentials. #privacy #certificates #academy #training
