World Teachers Day is the right day to certify the people who teach: courses delivered, hours in the classroom, the period covered. Instructors collect teaching records the way graduates collect certificates, and almost no school ever issues them one. Save this for Teachers' Day. Diplomy (diplomy.org) issues instructor credentials like any course. Send it to your program coordinator. #teachersday #instructors #certificates #academy
