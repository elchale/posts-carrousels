== IG ==
A course certificate needs a full name, a course, dates and an email to send it to. Identity numbers, home addresses and phone numbers belong in your enrollment file and nowhere near a page anyone can open. Keeping certificates minimal is the simplest privacy decision a school makes. Save this for your next data cleanup. Diplomy (diplomy.org) publishes only the essentials. Send it to whoever issues your certificates. #privacy #certificates #academy #training
