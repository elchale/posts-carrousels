== IG ==
Emails bounce, especially work addresses on a corporate course six months later. The certificate is not lost with the message: it lives at its own link and can be sent again through the course group, a phone number from enrollment, or a classmate. Diplomy (diplomy.org) keeps the link valid. #certificates #academy #training #admin
