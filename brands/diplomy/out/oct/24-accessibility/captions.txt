== IG ==
A certificate published as a picture is unreadable to anyone using a screen reader, and to anyone whose eyesight makes them zoom. Dark text on light, generous sizes, and a page whose details are real text rather than an image. It costs nothing and it covers more people than you would guess. Diplomy (diplomy.org) publishes real text. #accessibility #certificates #academy #inclusion
