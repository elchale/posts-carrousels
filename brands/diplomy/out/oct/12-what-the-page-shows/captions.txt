== IG ==
Students ask a fair question about certificate links: who can see this. The honest answer is anyone the graduate shares the link with, and what they see is the name, the course and its criteria, the school and the date. It is not a public directory anyone can browse. Say that clearly when you issue. Diplomy (diplomy.org) works this way. #privacy #certificates #academy #credentials
