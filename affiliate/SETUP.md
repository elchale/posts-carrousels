# SETUP — D0 to D30, in order

D0 = **Sunday 2026-08-16**. Dates below are absolute so you can tell if you're
behind. Nothing here needs a company, a tax ID, or a US bank.

Do the phases in order. The one sequencing rule that matters: **money rails and
program accounts exist before the social accounts, and the social accounts exist
three days before the first post.**

---

## PHASE 0 · Rails — D0, about 2 hours

Everything in this phase is done from the laptop, in one sitting.

### 0.1 PayPal (personal, Peru) — 20 min

`https://www.paypal.com/pe` → Personal account → your DNI, your Peruvian bank or
card. Confirm the email and link the bank so withdrawals work later.
**This one account collects Systeme.io, Hostinger, Fiverr and Temu.** No
business, no RUC.

### 0.2 Two Gmail accounts — 15 min

One per persona. Never the personal address.

```
account A → <handleA>.mail@gmail.com
account B → <handleB>.mail@gmail.com
```

Recovery phone: your own number is fine for both.

### 0.3 Temu affiliate — 15 min  *(lane A's engine)*

`https://www.temu.com/affiliate.html` → sign up as an individual creator.
- Traffic source: **social media / TikTok + Instagram**. A website is not required.
- Payout: **PayPal**, the address from 0.1.
- After approval, open the **Asset Center** in the dashboard. That is the only
  place you take product images from — see `COMPLIANCE.md`.
- Grab your referral code and your first product links now, even with no
  audience; you need them for the bio page in Phase 3.

### 0.4 Systeme.io affiliate + free account — 20 min  *(lane B's engine, and the bio page)*

`https://systeme.io/affiliate-program` → sign up (approval is automatic).
Then create a **free Systeme.io account** with the same email. The free plan
hosts the bio page, the lead magnet and the email list for both accounts, and it
is simultaneously the product you earn 60% lifetime on. Payout: PayPal,
minimum $30, paid the 10th of each month.

### 0.5 Hostinger + Fiverr — 20 min  *(lane B's second and third offers)*

- `https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates` — 40–60% per sale, PayPal.
- `https://www.fiverr.com/partnerships/affiliates` — 25% of a first-time buyer's
  order + 10% revenue share for 12 months; $100 payout threshold; PayPal or
  Payoneer.

Both approve fast and neither wants a company.

### 0.6 Payoneer — 15 min, **only if you decide to add Amazon later**

`https://www.payoneer.com` → individual account, DNI or passport. It gives you a
USD receiving account whose routing/account numbers Amazon accepts as direct
deposit. Skip this today if you're taking the Amazon-optional path in
`PROGRAMS.md`.

### 0.7 Handle check — 10 min

Check the same name is free on **both** TikTok and Instagram before committing.

Lane A candidates: `thefixlist` · `smallfixdaily` · `itfixedmykitchen` ·
`undertwentyfix` · `thecheapfixdaily`
Lane B candidates: `onepersonstack` · `thesolostack` · `runitsolo` ·
`solostackdaily` · `theonepersonco`

Rules: no numbers, no underscores, under 15 characters, says the niche out loud.
Write the two winners at the top of each playbook file when you pick them.

## PHASE 1 · The four accounts — D0 evening, 45 min

Create all four **while you are on the Raleigh Wi-Fi / US SIM**. The registration
region is sticky and it is the cheapest US signal you will ever buy. Never use a
VPN, before or after — a VPN looks worse to TikTok than a foreign IP does.

One phone for all four is fine. TikTok's account switcher holds both; Instagram
holds both. What gets accounts clustered is **identical content**, not a shared
device — so lane A and lane B never share a template, a caption style, or a
posting minute.

### 1.1 TikTok ×2

Sign up **with the Gmail**, not with Google/Facebook one-tap. Keep the account
type **Personal**. Do not switch to Business — since May 2026 that requires
company documents to keep the bio link and Business Suite, and you have none.

Profile fields, exactly:

| Field | Account A | Account B |
|---|---|---|
| Username | `@<handleA>` | `@<handleB>` |
| Name | `Cheap fixes for annoying stuff` | `AI stack for one-person businesses` |
| Bio | `the $10–20 fix for stuff that annoys you daily`<br>`everything I show → IG @<handleA>`<br>`#ad · I earn commission from links` | `tools + automations that run a business of one`<br>`free stack list → IG @<handleB>`<br>`#ad · I earn commission from links` |
| Photo | The product shot from your best find, cropped square | Flat black square, white monospace "1×" |

The **Name** field is searchable on TikTok — that is why it reads like a search
query and not like a brand. The bio link stays empty until 1,000 followers;
the plain-text IG handle does the work until then.

### 1.2 Instagram ×2

Sign up with the same Gmails. Then **Settings → Account type → Switch to
professional → Creator**. Creator is free, needs no documents, and gives you a
clickable bio link from day one. This is the only place your traffic can convert
in month one, so it gets the real link.

Then **create a Facebook Page for each and connect it** (Settings → Accounts
Center → link). You will never post on it. It exists because comment-to-DM
automation — the highest-converting CTA on Instagram — runs through Meta's API
and requires a Professional account with a connected Page. Doing it now saves
re-linking accounts later, which triggers re-review.

| Field | Account A | Account B |
|---|---|---|
| Name | `Cheap fixes · daily finds` | `One-person stack · AI + automation` |
| Bio | `the $10–20 fix for stuff that annoys you daily`<br>`new find every day`<br>`#ad · commissions from links ↓` | `the tools that run a business of one`<br>`free: 12 tools + 6 automations ↓`<br>`#ad · commissions from links` |
| Link | bio page from Phase 3 (leave empty today) | bio page from Phase 3 |

## PHASE 2 · Warm-up — D0 to D2 (Aug 16–18), 25 min per account per day

**Do not post anything yet.** For each account, on the phone, logged into that
account only:

1. Search the niche terms (A: `home finds`, `kitchen gadgets`, `organization
   hacks`, `things that fixed`; B: `ai tools`, `automation`, `solopreneur`,
   `side hustle`).
2. Watch 15+ carousels **to the last slide**. Swipe every slide. Dwell.
3. Like 10, save 5, follow 10–20 accounts in that niche.
4. Read and reply to 2–3 comment threads with something real.

This is what tells the For You graph which cluster the account belongs to before
it has any content of its own.

**Then keep doing it — 15 min/day per account, forever.** It is a habit, not a
phase, and on Instagram the early non-generic comment on a big niche account is
the cheapest profile visit you can buy in week one. What it cannot do is fix a
format problem: no amount of warming makes a carousel reach non-followers on IG
(see [`FORMATS.md`](FORMATS.md)).

Meanwhile, on the laptop: **batch 20 posts per account** (playbooks have the
templates and the first 20 subjects each). Twenty finished posts before the first
publish is what keeps the cadence when a day goes sideways.

## PHASE 3 · Bio page + lead magnet — D2 (Aug 18), 90 min

One Systeme.io account, two pages, one per persona.

1. Systeme.io → **Funnels → Create funnel → Build an audience (squeeze page)**.
2. Page A content:
   - Headline: `Everything I've shown, in one list`
   - Sub: `The 25 cheap fixes — what it is, what it costs, where to get it`
   - Email field → button `Send me the list`
   - Below the fold: buttons for the 6 most-shown products (Temu links)
3. Page B content:
   - Headline: `The one-person stack`
   - Sub: `12 tools and the 6 automations I actually run`
   - Email field → button `Send me the stack`
   - Below: the offer buttons (Systeme.io, Hostinger, Fiverr)
4. **Emails → Create the delivery email** with the PDF or a link to a page.
   Affiliate links inside the email are fine for Temu, Systeme.io, Hostinger and
   Fiverr — **never for Amazon**, which forbids links in email and in PDFs
   (`COMPLIANCE.md`).
5. Copy each page's URL into the matching **Instagram bio link**.
6. **Comment-to-DM automation**, both IG accounts. Free tier of ManyChat (or
   InstantDM / LinkDM) → connect the Creator account + its Facebook Page → one
   Quick Automation per account:
   - A: comment keyword `FIX` → DM: one line + the bio-page link.
   - B: comment keyword `STACK` → DM: one line + the lead-magnet link.
   Use ManyChat if it's the easiest to set up — but never *promote* it, it
   competes with Propaga.

The page is the only asset here that you own. Everything else is rented.

## PHASE 4 · Launch — D3 (Wednesday 2026-08-19)

### Cadence

| | TikTok | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| Account A | 3 carousels/day → 5/day, **+2 template videos** | 1 carousel + **2 Reels**/day + 2–3 Stories |
| Account B | 3 carousels/day → 5/day, **+1–2 videos** | 1 carousel + **1–2 Reels**/day + 2–3 Stories |

**The videos are not optional** — an Instagram carousel reaches ~14% of an
audience and 14% of zero followers is zero views, which is exactly what your five
brand accounts are seeing. Reels are the only cold-start surface on IG until 1k
(Trial Reels also require 1,000 followers — D30 list). Build videos from trending
CapCut templates using slides you already rendered; see
[`FORMATS.md`](FORMATS.md).

TikTok carries the volume because that is where your own five accounts get
distribution; Instagram stays deliberate. Set **DM privacy → Everyone** on both
TikToks (Settings → Privacy → Direct messages) — "DM me the word FIX / STACK"
works at zero followers and routes around the 1,000-follower link gate entirely.
Clear the request folder once a day.

Instagram carousels reach ~14% of an audience against ~31% for Reels, so on IG
the carousel is for depth and the Reel is for discovery. Lane A's Reels are the
same product stills at 0.8s each with a trending audio; lane B's are a 30–45s
screen recording of the automation actually running. Per-account detail lives in
[`ACCOUNTS.md`](ACCOUNTS.md) — read it before the first post.

Post times, US Eastern — stagger the two accounts so they never publish in the
same 30 minutes: **A at 7:10am, 12:40pm, 8:20pm · B at 8:30am, 1:30pm, 9:40pm.**

### Publishing checklist, per post

1. TikTok → **+ → Photo** → upload the 6–9 images from `out/<serie>/<post>/tt/`
   in order (1080×1920, keep text out of the top 150px and bottom 250px).
2. Sound: pick a **trending sound with under ~50k uses** from the Photo editor's
   suggestions. Volume low. No sound at all is the worst option.
3. Caption: hook line, then the disclosure, then 3–5 **specific** hashtags
   (`#homefinds` not `#fyp`). Disclosure must be visible before the "…more"
   cut — first 100 characters.
4. **The last slide is a send ask naming a person** — "send this to whoever does
   the dishes", not "save this". Sends are the only signal that ever broke your
   band (27.9K post: 289 shares; everything else: 0–6) and IG weights them
   highest for non-follower reach. See
   [`LESSONS-FROM-YOUR-DATA.md`](LESSONS-FROM-YOUR-DATA.md).
5. Post → immediately **pin your own first comment**: `DM me FIX and I'll send
   the list · also on IG @<handle>`.
6. Cross-post to Instagram from `out/<serie>/<post>/ig/`, same caption minus the
   TikTok-specific hashtags, link already in bio.
7. Stay 20 minutes and answer every comment with a question back. Early comment
   velocity is a distribution input; it is also free hook research.
8. **Clear the TikTok DM request folder** — that's where the leads are now.

### The daily loop, 45 minutes

```
09:00  post A#1 + B#1, pin comments               10 min
09:10  answer yesterday's comments on both         10 min
09:20  log yesterday's numbers in metrics.csv       5 min
09:25  produce 3 posts for tomorrow                20 min
       (midday + evening posts go out from the scheduler or by hand)
```

`metrics.csv` per account: `date, posts, views, likes, saves, shares, follows,
profile_views, link_clicks`. Saves and shares are the two columns that predict
which post to make three more of; views are the one that feels good and tells
you least.

## Moving back to Peru

Nothing breaks. The account's audience is set by who engages, and that is driven
by language, references and hashtags — all of which stay US. Expect a soft dip
for a week after the IP changes, and do not respond to it with a VPN. Keep the
US Gmail, keep posting on Eastern time, and keep writing prices in dollars.

## What is NOT part of this plan

- No follower buying, no engagement pods, no comment bots. All three are
  detectable and all three are why accounts get view-capped.
- No third or fourth TikTok account "to test more niches". Two is already at the
  edge of what one person can feed at 3 posts/day.
- No paid ads. Organic only, by your own rule.
- No reposting the five brands' Spanish carousels here. Different audience,
  different language, and it would tie these accounts to your real identity.
