# Programs — what pays, what it wants, what it pays into

Checked 2026-08-16. Rates move; re-check anything before you build a month of
content on it.

Legend for **Paperwork**: `none` = an email and a payout address, nothing else.

## Tier 1 — join in Phase 0, before the accounts exist

| Program | Rate | Cookie | Pays into | Paperwork | Join |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Temu** (lane A engine) | 5–30% by category + referral bonuses | code: order within 30d of redeeming; link: campaign-bound | PayPal | none | `temu.com/affiliate.html` |
| **Systeme.io** (lane B anchor) | **60% lifetime recurring** | lifetime, tied to the email not a cookie | PayPal, min $30, paid the 10th | none | `systeme.io/affiliate-program` |
| **Hostinger** | 40–60% one-time | 30d | PayPal | none | `hostinger.com/affiliates` |
| **Fiverr** | 25% of first-time buyer order + 10% rev-share 12mo; Logo Maker $30 CPA; Pro 70% FTB | 30d | PayPal / Payoneer, min $100 | none | `fiverr.com/partnerships/affiliates` |

Those four cover both lanes and none of them asks for a company, a tax ID or a
US bank.

**Closed or excluded:** Notion (closed to new applicants since Apr 2026) ·
ManyChat (Propaga competitor) · TikTok Shop affiliate (US creators only) ·
Walmart Creator (1k followers + US) · Binance (5k followers) · anything crypto
with KYC, which converts badly from cold social and risks the account.

## Amazon Associates — the optional one

Real numbers before you spend a day on it: **3–4.5% commission, minus 30% US
withholding** (Peru has no tax treaty with the US, so W-8BEN reduces nothing),
paid only into a USD account — i.e. Payoneer. A $15 item nets about **$0.32**.

It also brings the heaviest rules in this folder: no listing photos, no links in
email or PDF, no shorteners on top of `amzn.to`, and **3 qualifying sales within
180 days or the account closes**.

Add it only when lane A is real (D30+, 1k+ followers, consistent 10k+ view
posts), because at that point Amazon's *trust* with a US audience converts better
than Temu's, and the mix of both beats either. Steps if you do:

1. 10+ original public posts per profile, first — the review looks at them.
2. `affiliate-program.amazon.com` → Website & Mobile App list = the exact profile
   URLs, **not** the bio page.
3. Store ID = the handle. Content description: "home organization and small
   household problem-solvers via short-form carousels". Topics: Home & Kitchen.
   Traffic: social. Link method: SiteStripe.
4. Phone PIN → tax interview → **W-8BEN**, individual, no US TIN.
5. Payment: **Payoneer USD receiving account** entered as direct deposit. Do not
   leave it on the gift-card/check default.
6. Links: SiteStripe → Text → the `amzn.to` link, used raw.

## Payout rails

| Rail | Used by | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **PayPal personal (Peru)** | Temu, Systeme.io, Hostinger, Fiverr, most of tier 2 | DNI + a Peruvian bank/card |
| **Payoneer** | Amazon only | DNI or passport |
