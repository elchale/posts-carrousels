# Compliance — the rules that can end an account or a program

Read once before the first post, and again before adding Amazon. Each rule here
maps to a real termination path, not to caution for its own sake.

## 1 · Disclosure (FTC) — every post, both platforms

The disclosure must be **visible before the reader has to tap "…more"** — that
means inside the first ~100 characters of the caption, not at the end, not only
in hashtags, not only on a slide.

Use verbatim:

```
#ad · I earn commission from links
```

and, the moment an Amazon link is in play anywhere in that post's chain:

```
#ad · As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
```

Also true on the bio page and in the emails. It costs nothing and its absence is
the cheapest possible way to lose a program.

## 2 · Images

| Source | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Temu **Asset Center** creatives | ✅ — they exist to be posted |
| Photos you took of items you own | ✅ — and they convert best |
| Your own rendered text plates | ✅ |
| Amazon listing photos | ❌ **never** — must come through Amazon's own tools/API |
| Any seller's listing photos scraped off a page | ❌ |
| Screenshots of another creator's video/carousel | ❌ |
| AI-generated images of a real product | ❌ — misrepresentation, plus AI-label suppression |

If a product has no usable image, it doesn't get a post. There are ten thousand
other products.

## 3 · TikTok

- **Label AI content** when a slide contains synthetic or photoreal AI imagery.
  Text-only plates are not synthetic media and need no label. Labeled AI content
  gets less reach, which is a second reason lane A stays on real photos.
- **Personal accounts only.** Business accounts require business-document
  verification (May 2026) that you can't satisfy, and switching would cost you
  the bio link and the Business Suite mid-flight.
- No clickable bio link under 1,000 followers. The plain-text IG handle in the
  bio and the pinned first comment are the workaround; a text URL in the bio is
  allowed and does nothing, so don't bother.
- No follower buying, no pods, no comment bots — every one of them is detectable
  and view-capping is the standard penalty.
- No ads pointing at an affiliate URL. Organic only here anyway.

## 4 · Instagram

- **Never send a cold DM.** Meta's requirement is *user-initiated* messaging:
  automated DMs may only answer a comment, mention, story reply or an incoming
  message. Cold outreach is the top cause of restriction and shadowban, new
  accounts are capped near 20–50 DMs/day, and the cap is now a dynamic
  behavioural trust score, not a published number. Comment→DM and replying to
  people who wrote first are the only two channels used here.
- If you ever pitch several people by hand, never paste the identical paragraph
  — Meta's spam detection reads DM text.
- Creator (professional) account: link from day one, no documents.
- Same disclosure rules. The "Paid partnership" toggle is for brand deals, not
  affiliate links — the caption disclosure is what matters.
- Don't put a raw `amzn.to` link in an IG Story if Amazon is ever added; route
  everything through the bio page.

## 5 · Amazon-specific (only if you add it)

- Links may **not** appear in email, in a PDF, in an ebook, or in any offline
  document. This is why the lead-magnet PDF links back to a web page instead of
  linking out directly.
- No URL shorteners layered on top of `amzn.to`.
- Prices go stale: say "under $15", never "$12.99". Amazon explicitly polices
  price statements.
- 3 qualifying sales in 180 days or the account is closed. Those sales must not
  be your own.
- Amazon reviews the social profiles you listed. They must be public and carry
  10+ original posts at review time.

## 6 · Temu-specific

- Creatives from the Asset Center only.
- No brand misrepresentation, no invented discounts, no "limited time" that
  isn't. Their prohibitions cover misleading content and trademark bidding.
- Their referral codes are time-bound (redeem within an hour of installing the
  app). Don't describe the mechanic wrong in a caption — angry comments hurt
  more than the commission is worth.

## 7 · Claims — the rule that carries over from the five brands

**Cero falacias.** No figure without a source, no "studies show", no invented
testimonials, no algorithm claims. On these accounts the specific traps are:

- inventing how many people have a problem,
- quoting a price you didn't check today,
- "this saved me 10 hours a week" when you didn't measure it,
- describing a tool's feature you haven't personally used.

The correction is always the same: replace the invented number with the concrete
thing you actually did.

## 8 · Multi-account hygiene

Two TikToks and two Instagrams on one phone is normal and fine. What clusters
accounts and throttles them together is **similar content**, so:

- Different templates, different fonts, different palettes, different plate
  families for A and B.
- Never post the same carousel, or a re-skin of it, on both.
- Stagger posting so the two accounts never publish within the same 30 minutes.
- Two posts/day per account for the first two weeks, three after.
- No VPN, ever. A foreign IP is fine; a datacenter IP is not.
- If one account gets throttled, do not open a replacement — the fingerprint
  follows the content, not the handle.

## 9 · What would make this whole plan illegitimate

Stated plainly so it never happens by accident: don't fake a US identity to a
program that asks, don't file a W-8BEN with false residency, don't claim to have
tested products you haven't, and don't take a US-only program by pretending to
be somewhere you're not. Every program in `PROGRAMS.md` accepts you as exactly
what you are — a Peruvian individual with a PayPal account — which is why the
list is built the way it is.
