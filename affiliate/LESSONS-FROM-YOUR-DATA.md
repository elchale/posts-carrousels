# What your own 5 accounts already proved

Source: `data/tiktok-stats.json` + `data/TIKTOK-STATS-2026-08-15.md`, 31–61 posts
per brand across 5 Spanish accounts. It is the only real evidence in this
project, and it beats every article I could find. These rules are ported
directly into the two US accounts.

## 1 · Sends are the only lever that broke the band

`11-tiembla-sin-ti`: **27.9K views, 289 shares.** Every other post across all
five accounts has **0–6 shares** and sits at 100–800 views. Saves did not
separate anything — Propaga's swipe files got the best saves (13/8/6) and stayed
nailed at ~600.

Instagram's 2026 ranking says the same thing independently: *sends per reach* is
the strongest signal for showing content to non-followers.

> **Every post ends with a send ask that names a specific person.**
> Not "save this". Not "comment X". *"Send this to whoever does the dishes."*
> *"Send this to the person who says they'll do it later."*

That is the single highest-leverage line in the whole plan, and it costs nothing.

## 2 · Comment bait produced literally zero comments

"Comenta DEPENDE" was pasted across 12 published Qolca posts → **0 comments,
total.** So the IG comment-to-DM mechanic stays, but demoted and reworded: it
only works when the comment buys a *deliverable* ("comment FIX and I'll send the
list"), never when it asks for participation. If it's flat after two weeks, drop
it and put the link everywhere instead.

## 3 · The frame that wins is separation or revelation, never instruction

- Separation — *something happens and you're not there*: 27.9K, then 1,976 (4×
  band) on the drill day. The sibling post on the same topic without separation:
  **317**.
- Revelation — *this is true and nobody looks*: Radar's best are "there are
  tenders with **no bidders**" (788), "what your competitor sold the State is
  **public**" (638). Its procedurals ("without RNP you don't exist") land 100–200
  lower.

US translations for these accounts:

| Frame | Lane A (finds) | Lane B (stack) |
|---|---|---|
| Revelation | "The reason your drain clogs isn't hair." · "Your $200 vacuum is failing at the one job you bought it for." | "Everyone automates the wrong half of this." · "The free plan already does the thing you're paying $97 for." |
| Separation | "Your roommate does this every day while you're at work." · "What your car looks like to the person you're picking up." | "Your competitor answers in 4 minutes. You answer tomorrow." |
| Instruction ✗ | "How to organize your sink" — this is the shape that lands at band | "How to set up a funnel" |

## 4 · Borrowed dates cost you 80% of your reach

Qolca's three Día-del-Niño posts: **168 / 104 / 103** against a 550–620 band —
one fifth. ComeHomeTag's earthquake post multiplied by 50, because the earthquake
*is* its subject.

Rule: anchor to a date only when the date is the account's own pain. Lane A's
real dates are moving season, dorm move-in, spring/fall reset, and the week
after a holiday when the house is wrecked. Lane B's are Q4 planning, tax season
admin, and January "get organized" — not Halloween, not the Super Bowl.

## 5 · Pop culture is expensive filler

Zendaya / Marvel / Iron Man posts landed exactly inside band on every account and
below band on ComeHomeTag (121–206, its worst). Doesn't kill, doesn't save,
takes the longest to write. Don't.

## 6 · Calibrate your expectations from your own bands

31 posts on ComeHomeTag → **92 followers**. Bands of 100–800 views with one
27.9K outlier. The US consumer pool is far larger than Peru-Spanish niches and
lane A should beat those bands substantially — but the shape of the outcome is
the same: **a long flat band punctuated by rare breakouts**, and the breakout
comes from the frame, not from volume.

Volume's job is to buy more lottery tickets and to find the frame faster. It is
not, on its own, reach.
