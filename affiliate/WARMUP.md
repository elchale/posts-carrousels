## What to like and save

Like generously, but only inside the niche: every like is a vote about what the
account is. Save the posts whose *format* you intend to copy — saves are the
strongest "more of this" signal you can send about your own future content.

Never like anything outside the niche from these accounts. Use your personal
account for that.

## What to comment — templates

Comment **within the first 30 minutes** of a post going up on a big account;
that's where a comment gets seen by thousands of the exact people you want.

Rules: 4–20 words. Add information or ask a real question. Never a link, never
"check out my page", never emoji-only, never "great post". And never invent a
number or an experience — everything you type has to be true, same rule as the
five brands.

**Lane A** — casual, specific, from a person who owns things:
```
measured mine before buying — 1.5in is the size that fits most rentals
how's it holding up after a few months?
this is the exact thing I complained about yesterday
does it work on a textured wall or only flat?
the cheap version of this broke on me — is that one metal or plastic?
```

**Lane B** — an operator's remark, never a pitch:
```
the after-hours auto-text is usually the first thing worth setting up
what do you use for calls that come in while the crew is on a roof?
most crews I've worked with lose the job at the quote follow-up, not the call
does that sync back to the calendar or is it one-way?
curious what your no-show rate looked like before vs after
```

If someone replies to your comment, reply back. That's the cheapest profile
visit available and it costs one sentence.

## What not to do in these three days

- Do not post. Not one carousel, not one Reel. An account that posts before it
  has a cluster gets its first ten posts buried, and you only get one first ten.
- Do not follow-for-follow, do not use any growth tool, do not buy anything.
- Do not DM anyone. Not one person, either lane.
- Do not log in over a VPN.
