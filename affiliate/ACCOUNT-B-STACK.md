## The angle, and why it isn't "10 AI tools you need"

That post has been made four million times by people who have never run a
business. Yours has an unfair advantage the slop accounts can't fake: **you
actually operate this stack.** Propaga, Qolca and Radar Estatal are real
products with real automations behind them. Every post should smell like it came
from someone who shipped the thing, not someone who watched a demo.

> **Post = one task a solo operator loses hours to → the exact setup that does
> it without them.**

Concrete beats comprehensive. `The 20-minute setup that answers every inbound
lead while you sleep` outperforms `12 AI tools for entrepreneurs` because the
first one implies a person who has done it.

## Audience

US freelancers, agency-of-one operators, small local business owners, and people
who just quit a job to go solo. They are buyers — they already spend on tools —
and they think in hours saved, not features.

Not: students, "make $10k/month" chasers, crypto. Those get views and never
convert, and they drag the account's recommendation cluster somewhere you can't
sell to.

## Voice

Operator, not guru. First person, past tense, specific numbers about **your own**
work only.

- `I moved this to X and it cut the daily check to about 10 minutes.` ✓
- `Studies show automation saves 40% of your week.` ✗ — invented statistic, and
  the `cero falacias` rule kills it.
- Never money screenshots, never income claims, never "passive income".
- Name the cost out loud, including the free tier. Hiding the price reads as a
  pitch; naming it reads as advice.

## Slide template — 6 slides, all plates

| # | Role | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cover` | The task, framed as the hours it eats. No tool name. |
| 2 | `value` | Why the manual way keeps coming back — the loop, not the complaint. |
| 3 | `value` | The setup, in 3–5 bullets. Actual steps, actual tool names. |
| 4 | `stat` | The one number you can defend: your own before/after time. |
| 5 | `value` | The caveat — what it doesn't do, who shouldn't bother. |
| 6 | `cta` | `free: 12 tools + 6 automations → IG @handle` + disclosure. |

Reuse the Diplomy plate families (`cover` / `value` / `emph` / `cta`) re-graded
to a new palette. Suggested: near-black paper `#0B0D10`, ink `#F2F4F7`, one
accent `#5BE0A5`, mono display font. That reads as a terminal, sits opposite
Diplomy's blue, and — more importantly — looks nothing like account A.

## Post types, in the mix that works

| Share | Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 35% | **The setup** — one task, exact steps | `The 20-minute setup that follows up with every lead for you` |
| 25% | **The stack** — 4–6 tools for one job | `Everything I use to run a business of one — $0 and $60 versions` |
| 20% | **The teardown** — what you stopped using and why | `Three tools I cancelled this year and what replaced them` |
| 10% | **The template** — a prompt / SOP / checklist, given away whole | `The prompt that writes a week of client updates` |
| 10% | **The correction** — a popular bad take, corrected | `"AI will write your emails" — what it actually does is draft; here's the difference` |

The correction posts are the reach outliers in this niche. Disagreeing with the
consensus, politely and with a reason, gets shared by people who wanted someone
to say it.

## The email list is the actual product

Every post funnels to one download: **"The one-person stack — 12 tools and the 6
automations I run."** Hosted on Systeme.io, delivered by email, and every email
after it can carry offers forever, independent of TikTok's link gate and
independent of reach.

Send one email a week: one problem, one setup, one link. A 500-person list in
this niche is worth more than 20,000 TikTok followers, and it is the only part of
this plan that still works if both accounts get throttled.

## What good looks like

| Metric | Working | Broken |
|---|---|---|
| Saves / views | above 2% | below 0.8% → too general, name a specific task |
| Profile visits / views | above 2% | below 1% → the CTA slide is invisible |
| Email opt-ins / bio-page visits | above 25% | below 10% → the lead magnet promises the wrong thing |
| Paid conversions / opt-ins | above 1% | below 0.3% → wrong offer for this audience, swap it |

Views will be 3–10× lower than account A. That is expected and it is not a
failure signal — judge this account by opt-ins and commissions, and account A by
saves and follows.
